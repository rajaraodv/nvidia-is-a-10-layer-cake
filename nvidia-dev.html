How it works

One loop. Five moves. Forever.

01

Pick your path

Ten persona paths, each mapped to a layer of the NVIDIA stack and a real job market. A 60-second "what do you want to ship?" picker routes you.

02

Earn cores by shipping

Modules, projects, and capstones pay out cores — the points currency. Badges mark milestones; superbadges verify real deployed work automatically.

03

Certify, officially

Superbadges unlock official NVIDIA certification exams — proctored, hands-on, respected. Certs list you on the hiring marketplace.

04

Rank up, unlock perks

Cores + badges + certs move you up six ranks: Spark → Builder → Accelerator → Architect → Champion → Legend. Every rank unlocks real-world perks.

05

Multiply

Champions teach, host, hire, and found. Their output is the next builder's first click — that's the flywheel, and it's the entire point.
